What is GraphOps?

GraphOps is an incident-time graph reasoning engine: from "Something is compromised" to "Here is exactly what the attacker can reach, why, and what to contain."

Rather than a static graph viewer, GraphOps turns scattered telemetry into one incident picture, simulates attacker reachability via bounded weighted path propagation, identifies critical choke points, predicts the adversary's next move, and lets analysts test containment defensive actions before taking action.

Winning Demo in 4 Steps (90 Seconds)

  1. Preset 1: Select Scenario 1 — Compromised Workstation and hit Simulate breach. Watch the attack tree ignite hop-by-hop with shockwave pulses.
  2. Attack Paths: Click the Attack Paths tab. Both Crown Jewels (Domain Controller & Customer DB) fall at depth 2–3. Note the Predicted Next Move (DC01 at 87% confidence).
  3. Containment Lab: Click Containment Lab. GraphOps ranks defensive actions by % Risk Reduction. Observe Block SW-CORP (91% Risk Reduction).
  4. Verify Decision: Hit Simulate Action. Watch the graph partition: crown jewels exposed drops from 2/2 → 0/2, blast radius shrinks from 28 → 4. Zero guessing.

Defensible Engineering

  • Bounded Weighted Path Propagation: DP-based path score optimization incorporating edge attractiveness, technique weights, identity privileges, and asset criticality.
  • Explainable Edges: Every link has typed relationships, MITRE techniques, confidence, and technical evidence.
  • Zero-Install & 100% Client-Side: Zero external telemetry, zero backend storage, runs fully local in your browser.
